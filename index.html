OUR HERITAGE

The Island
of Gems

Sri Lanka — historically known as "Ratna Dweepa" (Island of Gems) — has been the world's finest source of precious gemstones for over 2,500 years. Ancient kings, Silk Road traders and modern collectors have all sought the extraordinary stones that emerge from our rich alluvial deposits.

Lanka Gem House was established in Ratnapura — the City of Gems — in 1986. For nearly four decades, our family has maintained the trust of collectors, designers and investors worldwide by upholding uncompromising standards of authenticity and quality.
