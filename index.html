THE LEGEND OF RATNAPURA

2,500 Years of Gemological Royalty

Sri Lanka (formerly Ceylon) is renowned across antiquities as Ratna-Dweepa — the "Island of Gems." From King Solomon courting the Queen of Sheba to the British Crown Jewels and Princess Diana's iconic engagement ring, Ceylon sapphires have adorned royalty for millennia.

At Blue Meridian, we operate an unbroken chain of custody. We partner directly with multigenerational pit-miners in Ratnapura and Elahera who practice non-invasive, artisanal gravel washing that preserves Sri Lanka’s lush rainforest ecosystem.

Eco-Restorative Mining

Mined shafts are backfilled and re-planted with native mahogany and tea foliage to maintain groundwater harmony.

Fair-Share Miner Royalties

Our extraction teams share in the final polished value of each gemstone, empowering artisanal mining communities.

Master Sri Lankan Lapidary

Every stone is hand-faceted by master gem cutters holding decades of intuition to maximize internal fire and color saturation.
